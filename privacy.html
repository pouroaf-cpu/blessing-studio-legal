Blessing Studio - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Blessing Studio is a private tool that posts the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

What we collect

When an account is connected, the platform gives the app an access token and basic profile details (account ID and display name). Nothing else is collected.

How it is used

The token is used only to upload videos to that account and to show which account is connected.

Storage

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are never sold or shared with anyone.

Removing access

You can remove access at any time in the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings, Security, Manage app permissions). Ask us and we will delete any stored data.
